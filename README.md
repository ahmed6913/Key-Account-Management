# 📊 Strategic Key Account Management — B2B Technology Portfolio

> A practical B2B Key Account Management case study covering account portfolio analysis, segmentation, account health scoring, revenue growth, retention, client communication, QBR reporting, and CRM opportunity management.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project demonstrates how a Key Account Executive / Key Account Manager can manage a portfolio of enterprise B2B clients from initial account analysis through relationship management, retention, revenue expansion, and CRM tracking.

The project is built around a simulated enterprise technology portfolio for:

**Future Technology Systems Co. (FutureTEC)**

The framework focuses on identifying high-value accounts, detecting account risks, prioritizing growth opportunities, developing account-specific strategies, managing client relationships, and tracking opportunities through a CRM.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective was to build a structured Key Account Management framework that can:

- Identify high-value strategic accounts
- Segment accounts based on business value and growth potential
- Measure account health
- Identify at-risk customers
- Develop account-specific growth strategies
- Identify cross-selling and upselling opportunities
- Improve customer retention
- Manage contract renewals
- Track sales opportunities through CRM
- Conduct Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs)
- Improve account revenue and long-term customer value

# 🎯 02 — Account Segmentation

Accounts were segmented based on their commercial importance and growth potential.

### 🟢 Strategic

High-value accounts with strong strategic importance.

**Management approach:**

- Executive relationship management
- QBRs
- Proactive renewal planning
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Long-term account roadmap

---

### 🔵 Growth

Accounts with strong expansion potential.

**Management approach:**

- Needs analysis
- Stakeholder mapping
- Solution workshops
- Cross-selling
- Upselling
- Pilot / PoC opportunities

---

### ⚪ Maintain

Accounts requiring efficient relationship and renewal management.

**Management approach:**

- Regular follow-ups
- Standard account reviews
- Renewal monitoring
- Customer support
- Identify opportunities when appropriate

# 📈 03 — Account Priority Matrix

A priority framework was created using:

- Revenue Value
- Account Health
- Growth Potential

### Revenue Value Score

| Annual Revenue | Score |
|---|---:|
| ≥ KWD 150K | 100 |
| KWD 100K–149K | 80 |
| KWD 75K–99K | 60 |
| KWD 50K–74K | 40 |

### Growth Potential

| Potential | Score |
|---|---:|
| High | 100 |
| Medium | 60 |
| Low | 30 |
